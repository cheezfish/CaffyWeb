Returns policy

Caffy FZC LLC · applies to orders delivered in the UAE

What we accept

CAFFY accepts returns of defective items reported within 7 days of delivery. We do not accept returns of items that are not defective.

How to return an item

  1. Email support@getcaffy.com within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a description of the problem.
  2. We will confirm the return and tell you how to send the item back.

Return shipping

CAFFY pays the return shipping for defective items. There is no charge to you.

Refunds

Once a return is approved, the refund is made in full to your original payment method within 5-10 business days.

Questions

Contact support@getcaffy.com or message us on our social channels. See also the FAQ and terms.
