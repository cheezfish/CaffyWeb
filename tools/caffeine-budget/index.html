Questions

Short answers, with sources below.

How much caffeine is in a CAFFY pouch?

About 80 mg per pouch, from guarana. That is about the same as a 250 ml energy drink can and a little less than an 8 oz cup of brewed coffee (about 96 mg).

How much caffeine is too much in a day?

The FDA and EFSA both treat up to 400 mg a day as not generally associated with negative effects in healthy adults. For pregnant women EFSA's figure is 200 mg a day, and it considers single doses up to 200 mg to raise no safety concerns.

How long does caffeine stay in your body?

The average elimination half-life is about 5 hours, so half of a dose is gone after roughly 5 hours. Individuals vary widely, from about 1.5 to 9.5 hours.

Does caffeine affect sleep?

EFSA notes that single doses of 100 mg of caffeine may affect sleep duration and patterns in some adults, particularly close to bedtime. The calculator shows how much is still active at your bedtime.
